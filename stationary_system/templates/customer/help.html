Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

How do I know my print is ready?

When the stationary confirms your request and marks it as completed, you'll see it in Activity history. You can also check the preview page of any activity.

What file types can I upload?

PDF, DOCX and image files (JPG / PNG). Up to 5 files per request, each no larger than 5MB.

How do I pay for a service?

Once the stationary sends the cost, open the activity preview and tap Make payment. You'll get the merchant's Lipa namba, submit your payment details, and confirm via WhatsApp.

Can I change the document format after submitting?

Yes — as long as the activity is still waiting or pending, open it from your history and tap Edit to update the format, files, or pickup point.

What if I submitted the wrong files?

Go to Activity history, open the request, and use Edit to remove or add files. If it's already completed, contact support for help.
